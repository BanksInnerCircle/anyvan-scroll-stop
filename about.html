Our story

Built on one big idea

In 2009, AnyVan launched with a simple but powerful insight: millions of vans drive across the UK every day with empty space. What if customers could fill that space — and save a fortune in the process?

That idea grew into the UK's largest transport marketplace. We connect customers who need something moved with trusted transport partners already heading in the right direction — cutting costs, reducing wasted journeys, and making moving affordable for everyone.

Today, AnyVan operates across the UK and into Europe, trusted by over 2.5 million customers and rated 4.6 stars on Trustpilot with 192,028 verified reviews.
